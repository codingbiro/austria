Ridgelines steal the last 30–45 minutes, so be in position ~45 min before the table time. If your dates include Aug 12: a partial solar eclipse is visible from this area low in the evening sky — that night’s sunset session doubles as an eclipse session (bring proper solar glasses).

  • Predigtstuhl meadow / hotel terrace 0–5 min — west-facing over the Goiserer valley, paragliders in the last light. Doorstep option, use it daily.
  • Ewige Wand gallery 5 min + 15 min walk — the cliff path glows and you look down the whole valley. Bring a headlamp for the walk back.
  • Hallstättersee west shore at Steeg / Untersee ~15 min — long evening light straight down the lake, far fewer people than Hallstatt.
  • Vorderer Gosausee ~35 min — 5 over the cap, worth it once — the sun sets behind you and the Gosaukamm / Bischofsmütze burns pink for ~15 minutes after sunset. The alpenglow evening here beats most sunsets.

Drone shots · Neo 2 / Mini 4 Pro

Rules first — this region is a minefield. Airspace is federal (Austro Control) but nature protection is state law, and flying in Naturschutzgebiete, national parks and Natura-2000 areas is mostly banned or strictly regulated — sub-250 g does not exempt you. Register as an operator, carry insurance, and check the Austro Control Drone Space map before every launch. Hard no-fly on this itinerary: the Loser / Augstsee circuit (nature reserve) and anywhere paragliders are up (Zwölferhorn, Predigtstuhl afternoons). Dawn is your window everywhere: calm air, no people, best light.
  • Vorderer Gosausee at first light (Day 1) — glassy Dachstein mirror, couple standing on the flat shore path; slow 20 m top-down or pull-back reveal. Verify in Drone Space on the day; land if anyone’s around. Mini 4 Pro
  • Predigtstuhl meadow at dawn — launch from the doorstep before the paragliders wake; on inversion mornings you get a cloud sea over the Hallstättersee with the Dachstein floating behind. either
  • Hallstättersee shore at Steeg — morning mist, low passes over the water; stay clear of the village and boats. Mini 4 Pro
  • Schoberstein summit bench (Day 3) — THE couple shot: bench, cliff edge, Attersee 600 m below. Wind on the edge gets sporty — quick orbit and top-down only. Save the Neo 2 for palm-launch follow shots on the trail below the summit. Mini 4 Pro
  • Ochsenkreuz bay (Day 3 evening) — turquoise shallows, two people swimming, golden-hour top-down. Neo 2
  • Gößl meadows at Grundlsee (Day 2) — open farmland, moody lake edge; launch here rather than the forested Toplitz shore, which edges the Totes Gebirge protection zone. either

Your screenshots — verdict & upgrades

In the plan: Vorderer Gosausee (Day 1), Loser / Augstsee (Day 2), Schoberstein (Day 3 — sent twice, message received).

Bleckwand (the rock window over the Wolfgangsee) is genuinely spectacular, but 12.2 km / 1 049 m out-and-back is a lot for day three of three. If your legs feel great, swap it in for the Schoberstein + Zwölferhorn combo — trailhead near Strobl, ~35 min from the hotel.

Spiegelsee / Reiteralm is the one to cut: the most photogenic of the five, but it sits on the Schladming side of the Dachstein — ~1 h 20 each way, nearly 3 h of driving. Only take it if the mirror-lake shot outranks everything; it would replace the Loser morning.

Two upgrades not in your screenshots: the 5fingers platform on Krippenstein (Dachstein cable car from Obertraun, 20 min from the hotel) — arguably the region’s single best viewpoint, hanging over the Hallstättersee with almost no hiking; perfect Day 1 add-on or bad-weather backup. And the Miesweg cliff boardwalk on the Traunsee from your saved list, which pairs with your saved Mostschenke im Heustadl (it’s in Ebensee — open Wed–Sat from 15:00, Sun from 12:00): together they’re the natural fourth day if you ever extend.

Practical notes

Book all three dinners a day ahead — small kitchens, and three of them are cash-only territory, so carry euros.

Start hikes by 09:00: beats the heat, the coach crowds at Gosausee, and the August afternoon-thunderstorm pattern.

Re-check closing days against your actual dates — Mon–Wed closures are scattered across these places (Rathlucken Mon–Tue, Fischerhütte Tue–Wed, Weissenbachwirt Mon–Wed).

Budget items: Gosausee and Weissenbach parking (paid), Loser Panoramastraße toll ~€20+ per car, Zwölferhorn return mid-€30s.

All drive times are approximate and include the hotel’s mountain road; add margin on summer weekends around Hallstatt.
