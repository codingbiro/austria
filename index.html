Salzkammergut → Graz · one road, two plans

Two trips,
one August

Lakes, benches and sunset dinners in the mountains — then a city, a gorge full of ladders, and dinner 400 metres above Graz. Pick a signpost.

Pick your days

Both plans share the house rules: slow starts, packed lunches on summits, dinner around 19:00, and a sunset run after if the mood strikes. Every stop carries a Google Maps link for the road.

TRIP 01 · 3 DAYS Alpenglow SALZKAMMERGUT · BERGHOTEL PREDIGSTUHL · GOSAU — ATTERSEE — TRAUNSEE — WOLFGANGSEE
≈ 30 km on foot · 6 lakes · 2 summits · 1 cog-railway option · 1 cuki bench 🪑 The mirror lakes of Gosau and the cliff gallery at home, a two-summit double over the Attersee and Traunsee, then the Zwölferhorn bench and a last swim at Ochsenkreuz. Sunset from the doorstep meadow, every night it's clear.
TRIP 02 · 2 DAYS Graz CITY & GORGE · RADISSON ANNENSTRASSE · EGGENBERG — SCHLOSSBERG — BÄRENSCHÜTZKLAMM — FÜRSTENSTAND
1 palace · 168 ladders & 24 waterfalls · sunset dinner at 754 m · gluten-free map included Eggenberg's planetary rooms and peacocks, the old town and the Schlossberg at golden hour — then the Bärenschützklamm ladder day, coffee in Frohnleiten's painted square, and dinner on Graz's house mountain while the city lights up below.
