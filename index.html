Drone shots · Neo 2 / Mini 4 Pro

Rules first — this region is a minefield. Airspace is federal (Austro Control) but nature protection is state law, and flying in Naturschutzgebiete, national parks and Natura-2000 areas is mostly banned or strictly regulated — sub-250 g does not exempt you. Register as an operator, carry insurance, and check the Austro Control Drone Space map before every launch. Hard no-fly on this itinerary: the crowded Schafberg summit (train crowds, no clean launch), anywhere paragliders are up (Predigtstuhl afternoons), and anything marked as a protected area in the app. Dawn is your window everywhere: calm air, no people, best light.
  • Vorderer Gosausee at first light (Day 1) — glassy Dachstein mirror, couple standing on the flat shore path; slow 20 m top-down or pull-back reveal. Verify in Drone Space on the day; land if anyone’s around. Mini 4 Pro
  • Predigtstuhl meadow at dawn — launch from the doorstep before the paragliders wake; on inversion mornings you get a cloud sea over the Hallstättersee with the Dachstein floating behind. either
  • Hallstättersee shore at Steeg — morning mist, low passes over the water; stay clear of the village and boats. Mini 4 Pro
  • Schoberstein summit bench (Day 2) — THE couple shot: bench, cliff edge, Attersee 600 m below. Wind on the edge gets sporty — quick orbit and top-down only. Save the Neo 2 for palm-launch follow shots on the trail below the summit. Mini 4 Pro
  • Kleiner Sonnstein summit (Day 2) — top-down on the Traunsee’s fjord bend with the Traunstein wall behind; the small summit gets a steady flow of people at midday, so launch-orbit-land fast, or fly from the shoulder just below the cross. Mini 4 Pro
  • Bürglstein shoreline, Wolfgangsee (Day 3) — couple on the rocky shore with the Schafberg pyramid behind; late-afternoon side light, calm water. either
  • Ochsenkreuz bay (Day 3 evening) — turquoise shallows, two people swimming, golden-hour top-down. Neo 2

Your screenshots — verdict & alternates

In the plan: Vorderer Gosausee (Day 1), Schoberstein (Day 2 — sent twice, message received), the Schafberg from your saved pins as the Day 3 postcard, and now the Traunsee too.

Why Kleiner Sonnstein won the Traunsee slot: three of your saved pins point at it (the 922 m peak, the hiking area, the Siegesbach parking), it’s the lake’s best effort-to-view ratio, and it sits 30 min from the Schoberstein trailhead — the only Traunsee highlight that genuinely pairs with the Attersee day. The runner-up, the Miesweg cliff boardwalk under the Traunstein, lives at the Gmunden end — 1 h+ each way — and pairs with your saved Mostschenke im Heustadl for a future Traunsee day instead.

Out by your call: the Loser / Augstsee screenshot and the Toplitzsee. They stay a ready-made spare day — if a storm front kills a hiking day, that loop (Loser circuit + fish dinner at the Fischerhütte, closed Tue–Wed) is the plug-in replacement.

Bleckwand (your rock-window screenshot) is the Day 3 swap for strong legs: 12.2 km / 1 049 m out-and-back from near Strobl. Zwölferhorn is the Day 3 fallback if Schafberg slots sell out — no reservation needed, and the cuki bench 🪑 is up there.

Spiegelsee / Reiteralm stays cut: the most photogenic of the five, but ~1 h 20 each way on the Schladming side of the Dachstein. It deserves its own trip. 5fingers on Krippenstein is pencilled into Day 1 as the add-on / weather backup — 20 min from the hotel, arguably the region’s best viewpoint for almost no hiking.

Practical notes

Book the SchafbergBahn now: tickets go online in advance at 5schaetze.at / ticketing.schafbergbahn.at — adult return to the summit is €61, the train runs daily April 25 – November 1, first ascents ~09:25, and August slots sell out. Book both directions.

Day 2 is the big one: two summits and ~1 050 m of climbing. Check the storm radar over lunch on the Schoberstein bench — if towers are building over the Höllengebirge, take Plan B. The Sonnstein is steep with a few secured steps; sturdy shoes, and its small lot fills midday (flip swim and climb if needed).

Book the dinners a day ahead — small kitchens, and Rathlucken Hütte and Weissenbachwirt are cash only, so carry euros.

Closing days to check against your actual dates: Rathlucken Hütte Mon–Tue, Weissenbachwirt Mon–Wed (Sun only to 18:00). Steegwirt is open daily.

Start hikes by 09:00: beats the heat, the coach crowds at Gosausee, and the August afternoon-thunderstorm pattern.

Budget items: paid parking at Gosausee, Weissenbach (6 h minimum), Rindbach (~€6) and the SchafbergBahn station; €61 pp for the railway.

All drive times are approximate and include the hotel’s mountain road; add margin on summer weekends around Hallstatt and St. Wolfgang.
