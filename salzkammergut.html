Three days from
the Predigtstuhl

Base: Berghotel Predigstuhl 1 004 m, above Bad Goisern at the end of a 6.2 km toll-free panorama road — Hallstättersee and Dachstein at the door. Slow mornings by design: out the door at 10:00 on Days 1 & 3 and 09:00 on the big middle day. Breakfast at the hotel (ask Coleen & Kevin for a Lunchpaket the evening before), picnic lunch on the trail, dinner lands ~19:00 — 19:30 at the latest — with the sunset run after, if the mood strikes. Drive times include the ~12–15 min mountain road.

Last sunset · terrace or meadow

In the mood for the postcard train instead? The SchafbergBahn from St. Wolfgang is the on-request upgrade: Austria’s steepest cog railway (since 1893), 35 min to the 1 783 m summit, Himmelspforte ledge and eleven lakes on a clear day. €61 pp return, daily April 25 – November 1, and August slots sell out — book both directions online days ahead at 5schaetze.at; with a 10:00 start aim for a late-morning slot. Seat tip: uphill, views start left and switch right near the top; picnic up there and skip the summit hotel restaurant. It replaces the Zwölferhorn block — Ochsenkreuz and dinner stay. Hungry for one more summit instead? The Bleckwand rock window: 12.2 km / 1 049 m out-and-back from near Strobl.

Drone shots · Neo 2 / Mini 4 Pro

Rules first — this region is a minefield. Airspace is federal (Austro Control) but nature protection is state law, and flying in Naturschutzgebiete, national parks and Natura-2000 areas is mostly banned or strictly regulated — sub-250 g does not exempt you. Register as an operator, carry insurance, and check the Austro Control Drone Space map before every launch. Hard no-fly on this itinerary: paraglider launch sites when gliders are up — the Zwölferhorn top station and the Predigtstuhl are both — the Schafberg summit if you take the train option, and anything marked as a protected area in the app. Dawn is your window everywhere: calm air, no people, best light.
  • Vorderer Gosausee at first light — now a before-breakfast mission (leave ~05:30 in early August): glassy Dachstein mirror, couple standing on the flat shore path; slow 20 m top-down or pull-back reveal. Verify in Drone Space on the day; land if anyone’s around. Mini 4 Pro

  • Schoberstein summit bench (Day 2) — THE couple shot: bench, cliff edge, Attersee 600 m below. Wind on the edge gets sporty — quick orbit and top-down only. Save the Neo 2 for palm-launch follow shots on the trail below the summit. Mini 4 Pro

Your screenshots — verdict & alternates

In the plan: Vorderer Gosausee (Day 1), Schoberstein (Day 2 — sent twice, message received) plus the Kleiner Sonnstein for the Traunsee, and the cuki bench 🪑 on the Zwölferhorn as the Day 3 finale.

Why Kleiner Sonnstein won the Traunsee slot: three of your saved pins point at it (the 922 m peak, the hiking area, the Siegesbach parking), it’s the lake’s best effort-to-view ratio, and it sits 30 min from the Schoberstein trailhead — the only Traunsee highlight that genuinely pairs with the Attersee day. The runner-up, the Miesweg cliff boardwalk under the Traunstein, lives at the Gmunden end — 1 h+ each way — and pairs with your saved Mostschenke im Heustadl for a future Traunsee day instead.

Schafberg moved to on-request: the postcard train is the Day 3 upgrade if the mood strikes — booking details sit in the day card, and it needs slots reserved days ahead, which is exactly why the walk-up Zwölferhorn makes the better default.

Out by your call: the Loser / Augstsee screenshot and the Toplitzsee. They stay a ready-made spare day — if a storm front kills a hiking day, that loop (Loser circuit + fish dinner at the Fischerhütte, closed Tue–Wed) is the plug-in replacement.

Bleckwand (your rock-window screenshot) is the Day 3 swap for strong legs: 12.2 km / 1 049 m out-and-back from near Strobl. Spiegelsee / Reiteralm stays cut: the most photogenic of the five, but ~1 h 20 each way on the Schladming side of the Dachstein — it deserves its own trip. 5fingers on Krippenstein is pencilled into Day 1 as the add-on / weather backup — 20 min from the hotel, arguably the region’s best viewpoint for almost no hiking.

Practical notes

Start times, baked in per your call: out the door 10:00 on Days 1 & 3, 09:00 on Day 2 — the earliest start goes to the day with two summits. Check the storm radar from the Schoberstein bench at lunch; if towers build over the Höllengebirge, take Plan B.

Late starts mean company: the Gosausee car park and the lifts run busiest 10:00–15:00, and the little Sonnstein lot (~15–20 cars) may be full at 14:00 — flip swim and climb if so. The Sonnstein itself is steep with a few secured steps; sturdy shoes.

Nothing to pre-book for the default plan. Only if you take the Schafberg train option: reserve both directions days ahead at 5schaetze.at / ticketing.schafbergbahn.at — €61 pp, daily April 25 – November 1. The Zwölferhorn takes walk-ups.

Dinners sit at 19:00 (your 19:30 ceiling holds everywhere except Rathlucken — order there by 19:15, kitchen closes 20:00). Book a day ahead; Rathlucken Hütte and Weissenbachwirt are cash only, so carry euros.

Closing days to check against your actual dates: Rathlucken Hütte Mon–Tue, Weissenbachwirt Mon–Wed (Sun only to 18:00). Steegwirt is open daily.

Budget items: paid parking at Gosausee, Weissenbach (6 h minimum) and Rindbach (~€6); Zwölferhorn return mid-€30s pp; Schafberg €61 pp only if you opt in.

All drive times are approximate and include the hotel’s mountain road; add margin on summer weekends around Hallstatt and St. Gilgen.
